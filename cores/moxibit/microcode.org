#+TITLE: moxibit microcode

moxibit runs every instruction as a short program of micro-operations
(µops) held in a ROM.  =ucoder.lisp= turns the tables below into
=moxibit_ucode.v=; run =make= in this directory after changing them.

A µop is one of:

- a *pass*: one clock to prime the register file's read ports, then
  32 clocks that stream every source through the datapath LSB first
  and shift every destination;
- a bus cycle: =read= and =readb= load DBUF[15:0], =write= and
  =writeb= drive DBUF[31:16], =fetch= loads the instruction register
  and dispatches on it;
- a =wait=: one clock, for testing a condition;
- an =inc=: ADR += 2, and a rotate of DBUF if its dbuf is rot16.
  It is the pass its other columns describe, except in a core built
  with FAST_INC when the sum does not carry out of bit 4: then it is
  done in parallel, in one clock or the 16 of the rotate.

ADR holds the PC between instructions, and an instruction begins with
ADR at its first byte.  Two bus µops are never adjacent: there is
always a clock with cyc low between two requests.

* Fields

Each column of the µop table is one field of the µop word.  The first
value listed for a field is its default, used for a blank cell.
Register names: A, B and C are the fields of the instruction at bits
7:4, 3:0 and 11:8; SR is special register (instruction bits 2:0);
fp, sp and r0 are $fp, $sp and $r0 (registers 0, 1 and 2); pk is r16, where the next PC is parked while ADR
is busy; t1 and t2 are r17 and r18, scratch; sr1-sr5 are the special
registers kept in the register file.  A register's code from 16 up is
its slot in the register file, so that decoding it costs nothing.

What the values mean:

- a, b :: the registers read on ports A and B.  w :: the register
  written, from wd: the ALU, the PC adder, DBUF[0], DBUF[8] (a byte
  on the high lane), port A, port B, the status word, or zero.
- mask :: zero- or sign-extend what is written above a byte or a
  short.
- adr, dbuf :: what is shifted into ADR and DBUF; =-= holds.  rot16
  rotates DBUF by 16, swapping its halves; shl shifts ADR left;
  shr1 shifts DBUF right by one.
- x, y, op :: the ALU's operands and operation.  i8 is the 8-bit
  immediate of inc and dec, o16 a 16-bit offset in DBUF with its sign
  extended, k the constant in column k, mb a multiplier step.
- pc :: what the PC adder adds to ADR: 0, 2, a taken branch's offset
  plus 2, or the constant k.
- roff :: half: port B reads half a turn round, so that a short or
  byte store finds its data's low half in DBUF[31:16]; lsr, asr, lsl:
  the direction of a shift (sp shamt, then shift).
- sp :: special behaviour; see =moxibit.v=.  caseq sets the
  condition codes as cas.l does: equal, or nothing.
- cond :: =c:label= goes to label if condition c holds and to next
  otherwise.  next must then be even and label must follow it.
  zero: the ALU's output (DBUF's, for mul) was zero for the whole
  pass.  irq: irq_i.  done: the divider's 32nd step.
- next :: the next µop.  =fetch= ends the instruction; =disp2=
  dispatches again on the instruction, after its immediate has been
  read.

* Dispatch

An instruction starts at =head=, chosen by its top eight bits;
=then= is where =disp2= goes once its immediate has been read.
Opcodes not listed are undefined instructions.  The rows marked in
=mdu= are undefined when the core is built with MDU = 0, so software
can emulate them.
